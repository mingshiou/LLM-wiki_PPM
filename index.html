Methodology & Curation Standards

Data Architecture & Reproducibility

This repository maintains documentation integrity and scientific reproducibility through structured conventions:

Persistent Compounding Knowledge

Knowledge is accumulated incrementally. New papers update existing pathogen pages, resolve historical nomenclature disputes, and expand effector catalogs without discarding baseline data.

Taxonomic Traceability

Captures taxonomic reclassifications (e.g. Xanthomonas perforans vs. X. euvesicatoria pv. perforans), providing synonyms and basionyms in YAML frontmatter for seamless search.

16:9 Reveal.js Presentation Standard

Educational slide decks are built in standalone HTML adhering to strict viewport budgets (1280×720), natural editorial typography, smooth cubic spline kinetic curves, and verified molecular topologies.
